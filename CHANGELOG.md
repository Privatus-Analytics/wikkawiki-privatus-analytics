# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-28

- Initial release.
- `privatusanalytics` action prints the Privatus Analytics tracking script.
- Settings in `wikka.config.php` for the site ID, the tracker modules (engage, auto, vitals, clicks, ask) and skipping admins.
- Works in a theme's `header.php` for the whole wiki, or inside one page.
